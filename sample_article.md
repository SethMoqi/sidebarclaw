# The potential role and mechanism of circRNA/miRNA axis in cholesterol synthesis

## Abstract

Cholesterol levels are an initiating risk factor for atherosclerosis. Many genes play a central role in cholesterol synthesis, including HMGCR, SQLE, HMGCS1, FDFT1, and DHCR24. CircRNAs may have longer half-lives and lower immunogenicity than linear RNA agents.

## Mechanism

Many studies show that circRNAs regulate cholesterol synthesis by regulating HMGCR, SQLE, HMGCS1, PTEN, DHCR24, SREBP-2, and PMK expression. MiRNAs are essential for circRNA-mediated cholesterol biosynthesis.

## Therapeutic Targets

Suppressing HMGCR, SQLE, and miR-122 with circRNA_ABCA1, circ-PRKCH, circEZH2, circRNA-SCAP, and circFOXO3 is described as a promising direction for drug development.
